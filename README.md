## The pitch

> The Spiteful Pillow: a pillow that goes flat the moment you finally get comfortable, then fluffs itself back up the second you give up and get out of bed.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes exactly that one sentence (154 characters, no trailing newline) to `entry.txt` in the output directory — deterministically, with no network access and no randomness.

Run it with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts` (Node 22+).

## Why this beats v1

v1 ("The Resentful Mug") scored 51.12% in round 1 (rank 1276/2735). Its mechanism — resentment reheating coffee — is abstract, and the payoff (warm coffee again) is mild. v2 flips the annoyance into the invention itself: the pillow's betrayal *is* the joke, a flat-when-comfortable / fluffy-when-you-leave reversal everyone who has ever fought a pillow recognises instantly, in one tight sentence with no extra clause.
